- null quantile = threshold not reached in 90 days, not zero
- species farms: equal six plots, seed at list price, starter capital explicit, no sprays/feed/breeding/discovery XP
- frequent checks each 5 min for 8 h/day; twice 12 h apart; daily once
- fertilizer route is one fixed random run and unrestricted supply, not a confidence interval
- life: daily six mixed plots, one qualifying solo-equivalent coop/day, explicit rare starter seeds, no guaranteed trait outcome; coins cannot go negative
- comparison does not measure human enjoyment or predict actual retention
